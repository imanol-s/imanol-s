## My contribution

On the Fall 2025 senior capstone team at UT Dallas, I focused on the reinforcement-learning training loops and simulation environments for a mobile robot's navigation policy.

I ported real-world environments into NVIDIA IsaacSim and helped define the IsaacLab training setup: observation and action spaces, reward shaping, and episode management.

## The problem

Training on physical hardware makes every failed trajectory costly. Resets take time, and obstacle avoidance needs many interactions. Simulation gave the team a way to iterate before physical deployment.

## The approach

The team used Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) for continuous control. IsaacSim provided the physics environment; IsaacLab organized training and parallel episodes on GPU.

The work also considered how continuous policy outputs would map to motor commands on the robot. That hardware-software boundary made eventual deployment validation an important next step.

## What this work shows

Working across unfamiliar tools, building a training environment, and connecting software decisions to physical constraints. Reward design and environment setup were central to the navigation work, alongside the choice of algorithm.

## schema

A dictionary is a schema for describing schemas. Every entry carries the same five attributes: the name people see, the name the system stores, the data type with its limit, what the field depends on, and its downstream proximity.

## lineage

A definition says what a field means. Lineage says what breaks when that meaning changes. Two attributes carry it: `dependencies` points upstream, and `downstream_proximity` records which consumers read a field and how many steps away each one is. Together they make every entry a node in a directed graph.

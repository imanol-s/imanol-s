## Where it is used

The dictionary became the reference other work is built on:

- A development team used it as the field reference for an internal data-maintenance application, including the business-facing labels in its interface.
- The data-quality validation rules were built from its attribute list.
- Templates for creating new records draw their allowed values from it.
- It is in scope for a proof of concept that brings governance documentation, quality rules and the dictionary into one application.

## What I'd change

Working from shared copies means merging changes by hand and depending on one maintainer. The next step, which I am now scoping, is moving the dictionary into an application where the governance documentation, quality rules and dictionary share one source and update together.
